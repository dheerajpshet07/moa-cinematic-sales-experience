# AI Visual Prompt System

These prompts define the visual language used by the local media generator and can be handed to Midjourney, DALL-E, or another image model for future art-directed replacements.

## Hero Video

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: fullscreen autoplay opening video  
Primary request: a cinematic night-to-gold flythrough of Mall of America as a retail-entertainment city under one roof  
Scene/backdrop: vast indoor atrium, skylights, glowing retail streets, crowd energy, event lights, luxury storefront reflections  
Style/medium: premium product film, high-end destination campaign, Apple/Tesla pacing  
Composition/framing: wide 16:9, slow dolly from abstract city grid into luminous indoor atrium  
Lighting/mood: black, platinum, warm gold, small hits of aqua and red, theatrical volumetric light  
Constraints: no logos, no readable fake text, no watermarks, no black frames
